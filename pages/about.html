About

Formed in Yellow Springs, OH by father-son duo Nerak Patterson Sr. and Jr., the NRPB (Nerak Roth Patterson Band) delivers brooding, emotionally charged blues and rock. Their sound blends crushing riffs and haunting melodies, drawing comparisons to Avenged Sevenfold, Alkaline Trio, Black Veil Brides, and HIM; with elements of B.B. King, Otis Rush, and Stevie Ray Vaughn while pushing boundaries with bold experimentation.

Nerak Sr. and Nerak Jr. began their career together while touring with Jethro Tull. Nerak Jr. was 11 years old then and was road managing Nerak Sr. At the age of 15 Nerak Jr. and Sr. would take the stage together at the AACW Music Festival in Yellow Springs, OH. 1 month later they would take the stage again at the Stranahan Theatre in Toledo, OH to open for the late B.B. King together. They would go on to open for B.B. King 5 times total.

The duo continued to write music together and tour the states including touring overseas to Italy and Switzerland. They continued to push musical boundaries incorporating rock into their blues, funk, reggae, punk, and even metal influence. They would then open for Saving Abel, Otep, and David Elefsons band Altitudes and Attitude.

They continued performing a lot until the pandemic shut the world down. Nerak Sr. almost died from covid so the duo left their band moved across the United States from Ohio to Washington State. They continued writing music together and recording together. They toured again in 2026 in Belgium and the Netherlands with Guy Davis.

Now, they are coming back with a vengence pushing the boundaries of music and redefining what blues can be.
